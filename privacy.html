Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What ReelForge stores

ReelForge runs only on your own computer. When you connect TikTok, it stores the TikTok access token and your TikTok display name on that computer, so it can send videos to your inbox. It stores nothing on any server and has no user accounts of its own.

What is sent to TikTok

Only the video files you choose to send, through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Sharing

ReelForge does not sell or share any data with anyone. There is no analytics or tracking.

Deleting your data

Disconnect ReelForge in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions, then Apps and services) and delete the ReelForge folder from your computer. That removes everything.
