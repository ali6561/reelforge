ReelForge

ReelForge is a desktop app that turns a creator's own livestreams into short vertical videos. It runs entirely on the creator's own computer.

When the creator connects their TikTok account, ReelForge sends finished videos to the creator's TikTok inbox as drafts. The creator reviews each draft in the TikTok app, writes or pastes the caption and decides whether to post it. ReelForge never posts on its own.
